# 🧠 IdeaForge — AI-Powered Collaborative Ideation Workspace

> **Think. Validate. Refine. Build. Together.**

IdeaForge is an AI-assisted collaborative workspace designed to help individuals and teams transform raw ideas into structured execution plans. Instead of being another online whiteboard, IdeaForge acts as an intelligent thinking partner throughout the entire ideation process.

---

## 🚀 Vision

Traditional whiteboards help people **visualize** ideas. **IdeaForge helps people generate, challenge, organize, validate, prioritize, and execute ideas.**

Our goal is to build a workspace where AI actively participates in brainstorming rather than simply answering prompts in a chat window. Whether you're planning a hackathon project, validating a startup idea, organizing an event, or creating a study roadmap, IdeaForge becomes your collaborative thinking environment.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Current whiteboard tools are excellent for drawing but poor at thinking. Existing workflows force users to:

- Generate ideas manually
- Perform market research separately
- Validate concepts using multiple websites
- Organize information manually
- Convert discussions into implementation plans themselves

This creates fragmented workflows and constant context switching. **IdeaForge solves this by embedding AI directly into the ideation process.**

---

## 💡 Core Idea

Every idea placed on the board becomes an **interactive knowledge object**. Instead of remaining static text, ideas can be:

- 🔍 **Critiqued** for risks and blind spots
- 💡 **Expanded** with new angles and adjacent concepts
- 📊 **Validated** against market trends and competitors
- 🛠️ **Converted** into actionable implementation plans
- 🔗 **Connected** with related ideas visually
- 📂 **Organized** into meaningful, structured frameworks

The board evolves from a collection of sticky notes into an **intelligent knowledge graph**.

## 🧩 Current Features

### 🎨 Collaborative Canvas

- Infinite board with zoom & pan navigation
- Real-time multi-user collaboration
- Interactive node creation, editing, and deletion
- One-click canvas clearing

### 🤖 AI Assistance

Current active AI actions:

- **Critique:** Identifies weaknesses, risks, blind spots, and improvement opportunities.
- **Expand:** Generates alternative perspectives, target user segments, hidden opportunities, and adjacent ideas.
- **Validate:** Performs competitor awareness analysis, market gap analysis, and feasibility evaluation.
- **Convert:** Transforms ideas into MVP plans, technical roadmaps, feature lists, and development timelines.
